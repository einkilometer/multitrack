Microphone Comparison

This recording session is designed to highlight the characteristics of different microphones. Each track contains audio captured with a specific microphone.

This recording can help to understand the specifics of microphones. To compare the microphones the soundfiles should be played in Solo. The files should not be played back all together. Each track contains the recording with a specific microphone. The jump-button starts playback from a device related position. The first recordings are done in distance of half arm length. With the Solo-Button you select the microphone you are interested in to listen to.
  • Solo Playback Required: To compare microphones effectively, play soundfiles one at a time using the Solo button. Avoid simultaneous playback.
  • Jump Buttons: Use the jump buttons to start playback from predefined positions that correspond to specific microphone distances or setups.
  • Recording Setup: Initial recordings were made at approximately half arm’s length from the source.
  • How to Listen: Select the microphone you're interested in by clicking its Solo button. This isolates the track for focused listening and comparison.
